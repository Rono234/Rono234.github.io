InkVoyage

A social media platform that connects readers and authors through feed-based book discovery and interactive profiles.

InkVoyage transforms traditional book platforms into a global social experience, allowing users to follow authors, interact with books, and build personal libraries.

Key Features:
  • Author and reader profiles
  • Book-focused social feed
  • Likes, comments, and followers
  • Personal reading libraries
Database Design:
  • Users, books, posts, comments, likes, followers
  • Dead authors with biographies and followers
  • User libraries (read / reading / want to read)
  • Search history tracking for analytics
